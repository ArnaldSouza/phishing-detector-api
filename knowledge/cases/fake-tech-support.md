# Fake technical support warning or call

## Technique
The victim sees a pop-up, email or phone call claiming the device is infected,
the account was hacked, or a subscription renewed. A "support agent" offers to
fix it and asks the victim to install remote-access software, pay a fee, or
read out account details.

## Warning signs
- An unsolicited warning that includes a phone number to call.
- A request to install remote-control software.
- Payment requested in gift cards, cryptocurrency or wire transfer.
- A message claiming to come from a well-known tech company.

## How to verify
Real companies do not contact users about infections or hacked accounts out of
the blue. Close the page, do not call the number, and check the account or
device through its official settings.
