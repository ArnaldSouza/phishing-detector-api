# Request to buy gift cards and send the codes

## Technique
The sender, posing as a boss, relative or official, asks the recipient to buy
gift cards and photograph or send the codes. Gift card codes are hard to
trace and impossible to recover once used, so they are a favorite way to
collect stolen money.

## Warning signs
- A request to buy gift cards "as a favor" or "for a client".
- Instructions to send the codes by message or email.
- Secrecy, or an excuse for being unable to talk by phone.

## How to verify
No legitimate organization asks to be paid in gift cards. Contact the person
through a number you already know before spending any money.
