# Message impersonating a tax, police or other government agency

## Technique
The message claims to come from a tax authority, court, police or utility, and
announces a debt, a fine, a pending refund or a legal problem. It pushes the
victim to pay quickly or to confirm personal data through a link.

## Warning signs
- A threat of legal action or arrest unless payment is made fast.
- A payment demanded by an unusual method.
- A link to a page that is not the agency's official site.
- A first contact by email or text, when agencies normally write by post or
  through their official portal.

## How to verify
Look up the agency's official portal on your own and check for notices there.
Government bodies do not demand immediate payment through an unsolicited link.
