# Refund or overpayment that must be returned

## Technique
The victim is told they are owed a refund, or that they were paid too much and
must return the difference. The message asks for bank or card details to send
the money, or for a transfer back of the supposed excess.

## Warning signs
- Money offered that the recipient was not expecting.
- A request for banking details through a link or reply.
- A request to send back part of an amount, often by an unusual method.

## How to verify
Check your account directly for any incoming payment. Real refunds go to the
original payment method and do not require you to enter card details through a
message.
