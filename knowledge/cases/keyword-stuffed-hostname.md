# Hostname stuffed with trust keywords

## Technique
The attacker chains trust words with hyphens to make the domain feel official,
for example combinations of a brand with "secure", "login", "verify" or
"account". The result is long and hyphen-heavy, while a real brand usually
owns a short, clean domain.

## Warning signs
- Several hyphens in a single label.
- Security or account vocabulary inside the domain itself.
- The brand name appears as one piece of a longer string.

## How to verify
Ask whether the real company would put "secure-login-verify" in its own
domain. Companies publish login pages under their main domain or a clearly
owned subdomain.
