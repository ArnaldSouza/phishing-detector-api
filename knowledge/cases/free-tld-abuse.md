# Domains on free or loosely moderated top-level domains

## Technique
Some top-level domains are free or very cheap and weakly policed, so they host
a disproportionate share of throwaway phishing sites. The attacker burns a
domain after a short campaign and registers another.

## Warning signs
- An unusual TLD on a site claiming to be a bank, a courier or a payment service.
- A domain that looks newly made, combined with urgent wording.

## How to verify
The TLD alone proves nothing, since many legitimate sites use unusual ones.
Treat it as one signal among several, and never as a verdict.
