# Brand name placed in the subdomain of an unrelated domain

## Technique
The attacker controls a registrable domain that has nothing to do with the
brand, often a free hosting platform where anyone can create subdomains, and
puts the brand in the labels to its left. Read left to right, the brand comes
first and the link looks official. The part that identifies the owner is the
registrable domain at the right.

## Warning signs
- The brand appears to the left of a domain that is not the brand's own.
- The registrable domain is a generic hosting platform or an unrelated name.
- Several dot-separated labels come before the final two.
- Words such as "login", "secure" or "verify" are chained with hyphens.

## How to verify
Read the hostname right to left. The last two labels (for example
`example.com`) name who controls the site. Every label to their left is chosen
freely by that owner. If the brand is not in the last two labels, the site
does not belong to the brand.
