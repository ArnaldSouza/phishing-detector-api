# Guaranteed-return investment or cryptocurrency giveaway

## Technique
The message promises high or guaranteed returns, a free token, or a giveaway
that doubles whatever the victim sends. The page asks the victim to connect a
wallet, deposit funds, or send a small amount first to "unlock" the reward.

## Warning signs
- Guaranteed profit, or returns far above what any real investment offers.
- An invitation to send money first and receive more back.
- Endorsement by a well-known person.
- Pressure to act before the offer expires.

## How to verify
Assume any guaranteed return is false. Check whether the platform is
registered with the financial regulator in your country, and never connect a
wallet or send funds to claim a reward.
