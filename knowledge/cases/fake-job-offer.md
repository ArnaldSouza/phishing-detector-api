# Job offer that asks for money or personal documents

## Technique
The attacker sends an attractive job offer, often remote, with high pay and no
interview. After a quick "hiring" process, the victim is asked for identity
documents, bank details, or payment for equipment, training or a background
check.

## Warning signs
- An offer for a position the recipient never applied for.
- Pay that is high compared to the work described.
- Conversation moved to a private messaging app.
- Any upfront payment, or a request for identity documents before a contract.

## How to verify
Search for the company's official careers page and confirm the position
exists there. A real employer never charges candidates to be hired.
