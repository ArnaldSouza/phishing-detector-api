# Urgent warning about account suspension

## Technique
The message claims the account will be locked, suspended or charged unless the
recipient acts immediately. Time pressure is the core mechanism: it pushes the
victim to click before checking anything.

## Warning signs
- A short deadline combined with a consequence, such as "within 24 hours".
- A generic greeting instead of the recipient's name.
- A button or link as the only way to resolve the problem.
- Sender address that does not match the organization it claims to be.

## How to verify
Do not use the link in the message. Open the service directly through its
official app or a typed address and check for notifications there.
