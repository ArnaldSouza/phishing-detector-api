# Malicious reply inserted into an existing email thread

## Technique
The attacker has already compromised one participant's mailbox. They reply
inside a real, ongoing conversation, so the message arrives with a familiar
subject and context. The reply contains a link or an attachment that is
presented as a follow-up to the discussion.

## Warning signs
- A link or file that does not match what the thread was about.
- A change in writing style, or a sudden request to open something.
- A reply that arrives at an unusual time or from a slightly different address.

## How to verify
Ask the colleague through another channel whether they sent it. A familiar
thread does not prove the sender is who they appear to be.
