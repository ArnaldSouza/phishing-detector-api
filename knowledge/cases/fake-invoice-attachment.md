# Unexpected invoice or payment notice with an attachment

## Technique
The message claims an invoice, receipt or payment confirmation is attached or
linked, for a purchase the recipient does not remember. Curiosity or worry
about an unknown charge pushes the victim to open the file or click the link.

## Warning signs
- An invoice from a company the recipient has never dealt with.
- A generic greeting and a vague description of what was bought.
- An amount large enough to cause alarm.
- An attachment or link as the only way to see the details.

## How to verify
Do not open the attachment. Check your own accounts and records for the
charge. If a company is genuinely owed money, it can be contacted through its
official website or phone number, never through the message.
