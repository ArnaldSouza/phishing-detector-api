# Parcel delivery problem requiring a small fee

## Technique
The message says a package could not be delivered and asks for a small
payment, such as a customs or redelivery fee, or for the recipient to confirm
an address. The amount is kept low so the victim does not hesitate, and the
real goal is to capture card details or personal data.

## Warning signs
- A delivery notice when no package is expected.
- A carrier name with no tracking number, or one that does not work on the
  carrier's own site.
- A request to pay a small fee through a link.

## How to verify
Open the carrier's official site or app and enter the tracking number there.
Carriers do not ask for card details through an unsolicited message.
