# Employee asking to change the bank account for salary

## Technique
The attacker poses as an employee and emails HR or payroll asking to update the
account where salary is paid. The next payment goes to an account controlled
by the attacker.

## Warning signs
- A request to change banking details sent by email only.
- A new account at a different bank or in a different name.
- Urgency before the next payroll run.
- A sender address that is close to, but not exactly, the employee's own.

## How to verify
Confirm the change with the employee through a known phone number or in
person. Require a formal, authenticated process for any change to payment
details.
