# Typosquatting with swapped, doubled or omitted letters

## Technique
The attacker registers a domain one keystroke away from a real brand: a letter
dropped, doubled, transposed, or replaced by a similar-looking digit such as
`0` for `o` or `1` for `l`. Victims who skim the link do not notice.

## Warning signs
- A brand name that is almost, but not exactly, spelled correctly.
- Digits mixed into what should be a purely alphabetic brand name.

## How to verify
Spell out the registrable domain letter by letter and compare it with the
brand's official address. When in doubt, open the brand's site from a
bookmark or a search, never from the message.
